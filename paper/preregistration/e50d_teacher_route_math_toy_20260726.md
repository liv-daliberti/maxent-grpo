# E50D — teacher-discovered, 0.5B-supported hard-MATH matched toy

**Status: PREREGISTERED BEFORE E50C TERMINAL RESULTS — 2026-07-26**

E50F was added to the fixed fallback preference when E50C had completed
teacher sampling for 8 of 50 problems and had emitted no validation,
clustering, menu, or 0.5B execution result.

Before E50C or E50F became terminal, the prospective E50F2/E50F4 hard-MATH
audits demonstrated false-new errors in their open-ended 72B relation
boundary.  They are quarantined as route-generation diagnostics and cannot
authorize E50D.  The sole eligible source is now E50G, which retains 72B
execution/soundness audits but creates canonical distinctions only through
the preregistered deterministic safe-signature gate.

## Activation and data

E50D activates only if E50G passes with ten genuinely distinct,
double-audited route pairs that Qwen2.5-0.5B executes both under forced
declarations and exposes both under 64-sample unforced generation.  E50D
copies those exact ten menus; it cannot repair, relabel, or replace them.

Training contains the ten selected dual-route level-5 rows from E50G and 40
nonoverlapping singleton rows from the frozen E49T toy.  If a selected row
duplicates an E49T singleton, the deterministic E50B filler rule uses the
earliest nonoverlapping E49T dual row pruned to its best frozen E49U route.
All 50 training IDs must be unique.  A separate no-gradient route-probe split
contains exactly those ten selected rows.

Task evaluation remains the same 50 held-out E49T MATH-500 rows, with the
nine inaccessible E49T dual menus pruned by the frozen E49U rule.  Strategy
IDs and listing order are explicitly non-preferential on every dual menu.

## Matched comparison and advancement

The comparison and gates are exactly E50B's preregistered contract:
Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, seed 45, 16 samples, learning rate 2e-7, exactly 150
updates/three prompt epochs, and one A100 per arm.  The control is ordinary
Dr.GRPO behind the shared answer-plus-route validator.  The treatment is the
unchanged E46 normalized canonical-bank Haarnoja method: novelty beta 0.50,
alpha 0.10 projected to [0.10, 0.50], normalized target 0.80, log-alpha Adam
learning rate 0.003, EMA 0.90, and no policy-entropy adaptation.

Use 64 unforced samples on all ten no-gradient route probes at shared base
initialization and at both terminal checkpoints.  Advancement requires
completed matched arms and identical step zero, nonzero gated reward in both,
the full E46 bank/controller causal chain, no more than a five-point task
loss versus Dr.GRPO, E46 route coverage no lower than shared initialization
or terminal Dr.GRPO, and natural support retained on at least eight of ten
E46 terminal probes, with at least two accepted samples per route and eight
accepted samples total.

Only a passing E50D can advance to exact 384-train/MATH-500 three-epoch
training.
