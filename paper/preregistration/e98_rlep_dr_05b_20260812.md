# E98 preregistration: RLEP-Dr on the Qwen2.5-0.5B collapse panel

Date frozen: 2026-08-12, before submission of any E98 collection or training
cell.

## Question and estimand

Does offline success replay, without semantic-mode balancing, alter the
accuracy/breadth trajectory relative to the completed E78 Dr.GRPO control? The
estimand is the paired terminal-pass difference, RLEP-Dr minus E78 control,
within domain and seed. This is a comparative baseline, not a component of the
paper's proposed verified-mode replay method.

RLEP follows Zhang et al. (arXiv:2507.07451) as a two-stage procedure: collect
verified trajectories from a prior converged RL policy, then restart training
from the original initialization and mix fresh on-policy rollouts with sampled
successes. The paper's collection and mixture constants are frozen here:
64 candidates per training prompt at temperature 0.7/top-p 0.95, retain only
verified successes, require at least two per prompt, then mix 16 fresh rollouts
with 2 replayed successes.

## Frozen adaptation to this campaign

- Model and restart initialization: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, exactly as E78.
- Experience-source policy: the completed terminal E78 Dr.GRPO `control`
  checkpoint for the same domain and seed. This is the prior converged RL
  policy; the untrained base model is not used to build the pool.
- Collection data: all 384 prompts in that cell's *training* split. Evaluation
  prompts are never placed in the replay pool. The launcher writes a
  content-identical read-only view under the evaluator's `multi_answer` split
  name and records a row-content digest; this is a split-label adaptation only.
- Collection: four fixed draws of 16 candidates, temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95.
  The collection seed is deterministically assigned from domain and run seed.
- Pool sampling preserves empirical trajectory frequency. It does not
  canonicalize, deduplicate, or balance semantic modes.
- Training domains: Graph Coloring, Python Factors, and PantryPlan.
- Training seeds: 43, 44, 45, 46, 47 (15 scientific cells).
- Training schedule: the E78 384-prompt, 8-pass Dr.GRPO schedule. Within each
  prompt update, advantages share one Dr.GRPO mean over the 16 fresh binary
  rewards plus two replay rewards fixed to one. The token/sequence
  normalization remains the campaign's Dr.GRPO convention rather than RLEP's
  original GRPO standard-deviation normalization; we therefore call this arm
  RLEP-Dr.
- Canonical replay and verified-mode discovery tracking are disabled. Thus the
  RLEP replay gradient and its two additional rows are the active intervention;
  it is not compute-matched to E78's passive replay traversal.

## Hard gates

Each seed-specific pool must contain exactly one complete four-draw sidecar,
cover exactly 384 identical prompt references on every draw, and contain at
least two verified trajectories for every prompt. Pool audit failure blocks the
dependent scientific cell; prompts may not be dropped and trajectories may not
be borrowed across seeds or domains. A 32-query Graph/s43 learner smoke depends
on its audited pool, and all 15 scientific cells additionally depend on that
smoke. Collection and smoke jobs are operational artifacts, not observations.

## Outcomes and reporting

Use the campaign's registered terminal accuracy and verified-breadth outcomes
and report all completed seeds, paired to E78 control. Also report pool size and
minimum/maximum successes per prompt plus the mixed-batch reward mean and replay
gradient diagnostics. Do not tune replay count, collection decoding, or seed
policy after observing these runs. Infrastructure retries must use the frozen
configuration; an eligibility failure is a substantive feasibility result.
