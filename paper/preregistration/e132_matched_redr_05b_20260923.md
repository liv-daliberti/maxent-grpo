# E132 preregistration: Re:Dr on the E128-controlled panel

Date frozen: 2026-09-23, before submission of any E132 cell.

## The question this answers

E130 and E131 are both measured against the E128 control, which ran on `cs`
A5000 nodes 203 and 204 under one evaluator. The Re:Dr arm they are compared
against is not from that cohort: it is the published E78 replay arm, whose
cells span node302 (A100) and node105 (A5000), so it is not even GPU-model
consistent within itself. Every statement of the form "capacity-one recovers
57% of the Re:Dr effect" therefore pairs a within-cohort numerator against a
cross-cohort denominator.

For diversity the seam is demonstrably small. The comparator payload records
the E128-minus-E78 control shift over 20 paired cells as a mean of $+.0028$
terminal PCMD, and recomputing E130's recovered fraction against E78's own
control rather than E128's moves it by less than one point --- 57% either way.
For correctness the seam is not small. The two controls differ by more than
ten points of `pass@8` in two domains, `Countdown` (.589 against .477) and
`Python` (.338 against .172), so E130's accuracy recovery is 87% against one
control and 77% against the other, and neither number is a within-cohort
quantity.

E132 removes the seam by running Re:Dr itself on the E128 panel.

## What moves

Exactly two keys move from the E128 control objective: the variant becomes
`verified_first_replay_rehearsal_only`, and the replay derivative is switched
on (`COMPUTE_ONLY=0`). Bank capacity is **not** restated. The default is 16,
which is Re:Dr's capacity, and the launcher's drift guard treats silence as
the stronger assertion that a key is untouched. E132 is therefore the same
cohort as E130 with one difference --- E130 forces the capacity to one, E132
leaves it at Re:Dr's value.

## Registered predictions

1. **Diversity agreement.** Terminal PCMD for E132 falls within $.05$ of the
   published E78 Re:Dr value of $.376$ on the five-domain mean. This is the
   prediction that the cross-cohort seam was benign for diversity, and it is
   the one that licenses every PCMD comparison already reported.
2. **Correctness disagreement is permitted.** Terminal `pass@8` for E132 is
   **not** predicted to match the published $.748$. The controls differ
   materially on correctness, so a shift of either sign is expected and is
   not evidence against Re:Dr. What is registered is the weaker claim that
   E132 `pass@8` exceeds its matched E128 control on the five-domain mean.
3. **The ablation conclusion survives.** Recomputed entirely within cohort,
   the capacity-one PCMD effect remains below the E132 effect, and the
   recovered fraction stays strictly between 25% and 85%. Disconfirmation ---
   a within-cohort recovered fraction near zero or near one --- would mean the
   mechanism statement in Sec. 5 rests on the cohort seam rather than on the
   ablation, and it would have to be restated from E132 rather than defended.

Predictions 1 and 3 are the ones that can overturn something already written.
Prediction 2 is registered so that an accuracy shift is read as a property of
the control panel rather than as a failure of replay.

## Frozen cohort

- Model and initialization: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, exactly as E128 and E130.
- Objective: `e78.fixed_objective("control")` with the two keys above and no
  others; the launcher refuses a third.
- Domains: Graph Coloring, Countdown, PythonFactors, MathIR, PantryPlan.
- Seeds: 43, 44, 45, 46, 47 (25 scientific cells).
- Training: 384 prompts, 8 passes, 16 rollouts per prompt, learning rate
  2e-7, beta=0, one PPO epoch, and the E78 decoding/evaluation schedule.
- Runtime: the snapshot E130 ran under,
  `diversity_comparators_40628bb13a366baa`. Its one declared difference from
  the E126--E128 snapshot is the capacity-floor relaxation, which `args.py`
  makes inert for any capacity of two or more. At capacity 16 that hunk is
  unreachable, so E132 is runtime-identical to the control it is paired with.
- Placement: `cs` partition, `allcs` account, A5000, node203 or node204 ---
  the same nodes E128 and E130 used, enforced by the shared launcher rather
  than restated here. `mltheory`'s A5000, node105, is excluded: it was
  draining at submission time, and it is one of the nodes the published E78
  arm ran on, which is part of what E132 exists to get away from.
- Control: the completed E128 cells, paired within domain and seed.

## No source change

E132 declares no source change. The capacity floor relaxation E130 required is
already in the snapshot and is unreachable at capacity 16.
