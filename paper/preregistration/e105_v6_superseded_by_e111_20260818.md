## Decision

Existing partial run artifacts remain immutable for auditability, but E105 must not be reported as the corrected MaxEnt treatment and must not be silently pooled with its replacement.

If E111 fails, E105 remains invalid as a test of the corrected mechanism; the failure determines the next implementation repair rather than reviving v6.

E109 is not superseded or canceled. It remains the valid repaired-Python ReplayDr direct-comparator cohort.
