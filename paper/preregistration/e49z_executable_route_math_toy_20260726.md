# E49Z — executable-route hard-MATH E46 toy

**Status: PREREGISTERED BEFORE MATERIALIZATION — 2026-07-26**

## Advancement gate

E49Z may be materialized only if E49Y passes its frozen requirement of ten
bidirectionally executable level-5 training problems.  E49T's completed
matched result is terminal evidence but is not eligible for full scaling:
its treatment route coverage was 0.30 versus 0.38 for Dr.GRPO and neither arm
fully covered any of ten dual evaluation menus.

## Frozen data construction

Training contains exactly 50 hard MATH prompts:

- retain byte-for-byte at the finite-menu level the 40 singleton E49T
  training problems in their original order;
- discard all ten E49T dual training menus that E49U found inaccessible; and
- append the deterministic first ten bidirectionally executable E49Y
  level-5 problems in E49Y's frozen candidate order, using their
  double-audited compiled menus and exact source rows from
  `math12k_384_math500`.

Evaluation retains the same 50 held-out E49T MATH-500 problems.  Every
singleton menu remains unchanged.  A dual menu remains dual only when E49U
proved it bidirectionally executable.  Every other dual menu is pruned to the
route with the most fully gated E49U successes; ties choose S1.  Pruning
copies the exact retained operations, renumbers only their action IDs, and
changes no mathematics.  The final support must be ten dual training menus
and one dual evaluation menu.  All 100 menus must parse, bind to the original
answers, and fit the 2,048-token prompt limit.

## Matched comparison

Run Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct seed 45 for exactly three prompt epochs, 16 samples
per problem, learning rate 2e-7, and one A100 per arm:

- control: ordinary Dr.GRPO behind the shared answer-plus-route gate;
- treatment: the unchanged current E46 normalized canonical-bank Haarnoja
  method with novelty beta 0.50, alpha 0.10 projected to [0.10, 0.50],
  normalized target 0.80, log-alpha Adam learning rate 0.003, and EMA 0.90.

Policy-entropy adaptation remains disabled.  Advancement requires exact
matched initialization; nonzero answer-plus-route reward in both arms;
treatment support at least two; nonzero normalized-entropy observations and
signed Haarnoja optimizer steps; no greater than five-point terminal loss in
greedy or sampled mean versus control; and frozen 72B terminal route coverage
that is preserved or improved.  The route-aware evaluation must explicitly
report the one certified dual evaluation prompt rather than silently treating
answer-equivalent text as strategy diversity.
