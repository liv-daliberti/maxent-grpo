# E47K-CAL: reasoning-integrity MATH strategy calibration

**Status: FROZEN BEFORE LAUNCH — 2026-07-24**

## Reason for the prospective successor

E47J completed all 50 bounded problems, made no structural error, produced
zero false-new events on 150 exact/format/lexical controls, and assigned keys
to 230/275 (83.64%) answer-matched policy responses. Its blinded manual audit
nevertheless failed the different-route gate: Qwen72 merged a direct sign
contradiction with an expanded absolute-value case/discriminant route. The
auditor labeled those routes different before opening the hidden judge label.
E47J is preserved as a calibration failure and cannot launch E49.

The failure also exposed misleading language in the E47J judge prompt. The
ordinary MATH validator establishes final-answer equivalence; it does not
certify every written inference. Calling its inputs “externally validated
correct solutions” encouraged the semantic judge to excuse incoherent
derivations.

## Prospective change

E47K changes only the semantic judge contract. The data, 50 problems, 3,200
Qwen0.5B samples, validator outcomes, blinded controls, online group-16
schedule, Qwen2.5-72B-AWQ checkpoint, two permutation seeds, temperature-zero
decoding, finite membership-only JSON schema, component-incidence admission
rule, and numerical gates are unchanged.

The prompt now states that every candidate is merely answer-matched. Before
clustering, the judge must put a response in `ambiguous_ids` if the written
derivation contains a material false inference, leaves a necessary case
unhandled, contradicts itself, or otherwise fails to establish the answer.
The judge may not silently repair a response or infer a proof it did not
write. Two independently permuted passes must both retain the response; one
integrity rejection fails closed and earns no key.

For retained responses, the same essential-route rule applies. Rewording,
notation, routine algebra order, and expanded or omitted routine steps remain
equivalent. Different central constructions, theorems, substitutions,
invariants, case decompositions, counting arguments, or proof routes remain
different. The prompt gives general boundary examples: a direct
sign/bound/invariant contradiction differs from expansion followed by cases,
roots, or discriminants; synthetic differs from coordinate construction; and
direct differs from complementary counting. These examples instantiate the
already-frozen definition rather than changing the downstream objective.

The persistent executable schema is
`math_strategy_canonicalizer_integrity_incidence_v9`. Calibration and any
later training import the identical implementation.

## Frozen gate

E47K advances only if:

- exact-duplicate false-new is zero;
- overall injected false-new is at most 5%;
- lexical-paraphrase false-new is at most 10%;
- at least 80% of answer-matched policy responses receive a conservative key;
- a newly constructed blinded manual audit has same-pair false-new at most 5%
  and different-pair false-merge at most 20%; and
- every bounded problem completes without structural failure.

The audit labels are completed before opening E47K judge labels. Passing
establishes only a sampled, answer-bound and reasoning-screened strategy key.
It does not make Qwen72 a formal proof checker or establish exhaustive
strategy support.
