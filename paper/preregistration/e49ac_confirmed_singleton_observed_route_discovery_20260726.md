## Activation and rationale

E49AC may run only if E49AB does not yield ten bidirectionally executable
menus under its repeated-component requirement.  It does not alter E49AA or
E49AB and does not reinterpret their failures.

The online canonical bank necessarily observes every genuinely new outcome
as a singleton on its first occurrence.  Requiring two natural occurrences
before even testing a route is therefore stronger than the online discovery
semantics.  In terminal E49AA, the calibrated E47W replay admitted 659 of
858 checked responses and created 66 first-occurrence strategy keys, but no
problem acquired a second distinct repeated key.

E49AC permits an observed singleton component only after additional
independent evidence.  This changes the support-count requirement, not the
answer validator, derivation validator, semantic relation, finite-menu, or
execution gates.

## Menu and stronger executability gate

Convert only the two observed components into an S1/S2 finite action menu,
supplying up to the first three response-SHA-ordered members of each key.
Two independent Qwen72 audits must establish mathematical soundness,
sufficiency, exact exemplar binding, genuine route distinctness, and no
answer leakage.

For each double-audited menu, force Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct to attempt each
route 64 times with a 3,072-token model context and at most 1,024 generated
tokens, matching the downstream training context.  A success requires:

1. exact final-answer validation;
2. unanimous assignment to the requested finite-menu route by the frozen
   E49T canonicalizer; and
3. no assignment to the other route.

Both routes must achieve at least two fully gated successes out of 64.
The higher bar prevents one accidental singleton from licensing a training
menu.  Wrong-route or answer-only responses do not count.

For every menu that passes forced execution, also draw 64 independent
unforced samples from the same base model with strategy IDs and listing
order explicitly marked non-preferential.  A problem retains natural
support only when each route has at least two exact-answer-plus-route
successes and at least eight samples are accepted in total.  Rank retained
problems by decreasing smaller unforced-route count, decreasing total
unforced accepted count, decreasing smaller observed-component support, then
source index.  The later E50B baseline probe is a separate draw and remains
an independent gate.

E49AC passes only with ten such forced-executable and naturally supported
level-4/5 problems.  Select the first ten under the frozen rank.  No route
may be relabeled, repaired, simplified, or promoted after forced or
unforced outcomes are seen.
