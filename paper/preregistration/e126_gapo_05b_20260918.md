# E126 preregistration: GAPO on the Qwen2.5-0.5B collapse panel

Date frozen: 2026-09-18, before submission of any E126 scientific cell.

## Question and estimand

Does Group-Aware Policy Optimization (GAPO) preserve verified-answer breadth
better than a matched Dr.GRPO compute-matched control while retaining
comparable accuracy? The estimand is the paired terminal-pass difference, GAPO
minus the E128 control (see below), within domain and seed. Intermediate half-pass checkpoints
are trajectories, not additional independent samples.

GAPO follows Anschel et al. (EMNLP 2025). A verifier-positive rollout carries

    Rtilde_i = 1 - (f_i - 1/L)

where `f_i` is the row's empirical frequency among the group's valid rows and
`L` is the size of the prompt's valid response set; a verifier-negative rollout
carries `-1`. Rows collapse onto one another through the task grader's
canonical answer key, so two strings certifying the same mode are one valid
completion rather than two.

GAPO is the closest published analogue to this campaign's own pressure: both
refuse to let an already-produced mode keep its full weight. What the
comparison isolates is where that refusal acts. GAPO flattens frequency inside
the fresh rollout group; the replay methods equalize rehearsal across a bank of
verified modes over time.

## Two declared departures from the published setting

**`L` is read, not estimated.** GAPO assumes a known valid set `V`. ModeBench
certifies one, so `L` is the prompt's `answer_mode_count`, served from a frozen
index (`var/artifacts/gapo_support_index.json`, 2688 prompts, pinned by digest
in the cohort ledger) and joined on a digest of the prompt's reference payload.
A lookup miss is a mechanism failure, never a defaulted support. This is the
faithful reading of the paper's assumption and is the reason this comparison
can be run here at all; it is not available to a practitioner without an
enumerated benchmark, and the paper will say so.

**The reward is affinely rescaled onto the control's unit span.** The published
reward spans `[-1, 1]` and rides GRPO, whose advantage divides by the group
standard deviation and is therefore scale-free. This campaign's backbone is
Dr.GRPO, which does not divide by that standard deviation, so the raw span
would change the effective step size relative to the paired control and
confound the objective with the learning rate. Cells run
`(Rtilde + 1) / 2`. Affine maps commute with group centering up to the shared
factor, so GAPO's relative structure is preserved exactly; an invalid row lands
on the binary control's zero. The unscaled span remains selectable and is not
what these cells run.

## A registered expectation about two domains

GAPO's `1/L` term expresses a uniform target over the valid set. With sixteen
rollouts per prompt, that target is reachable only where `L` is comparable to
the group size. On the train splits: Countdown `L` in [2, 8], MathIR `L = 5`,
and Graph Coloring `L` in [4, 18] with 0.3% of prompts above sixteen;
PantryPlan `L` in [8, 45] with 53% above sixteen; PythonFactors `L` in
[16, 3600], median 140, with 95% above sixteen.

Where `L` greatly exceeds the group size the `1/L` term is numerically inert
and GAPO's reward degenerates to `1 - f_i`, a pure within-group duplicate
penalty. This is a property of the domain meeting the method, not a defect, and
it is registered here so that a weak PythonFactors effect is read as the
predicted consequence of an unreachable uniform target rather than discovered
afterwards. The per-batch fraction of groups whose support exceeds the group
size is recorded as a diagnostic throughout training.

## Frozen cohort

- Model and initialization: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, exactly as E78.
- Domains: Graph Coloring, Countdown, PythonFactors, MathIR, PantryPlan.
- Seeds: 43, 44, 45, 46, 47 (25 scientific cells).
- Training: 384 prompts, 8 passes, 16 rollouts per prompt, learning rate 2e-7,
  beta=0, one PPO epoch, and the E78 decoding/evaluation schedule.
- Prompt surface, datasets, verifier, and all non-objective settings are
  inherited cell-by-cell from E78's registered configuration.
- Comparator: the E128 `control` cell for the same domain and seed, trained
  under this cohort's runtime, placement, and evaluator.
- Compute envelope: the passive, zero-derivative canonical replay traversal
  used by the E78 control remains enabled. GAPO's reward substitution is the
  only live-gradient difference. The outcome-collision bonus is refused while
  GAPO is active, because it penalizes the same within-group frequency twice.

## The comparator is E128, not E78

These cells are differenced against **E128**, a matched Dr.GRPO control re-run
under this cohort's runtime, placement, and evaluator, not against the
completed E78 control that E97 and E115 used. Three things prevent inheriting
E78, and E128 removes all three:

- **Runtime.** E78 pins `e76_tuned_scale_96f68ebb47757af8`, retired by the
  irreversible 2026-09-04 cleanup of 528 unreferenced source snapshots and not
  reproduced by any commit in the repository's history. It cannot be rebuilt,
  and its distance from any present-day runtime cannot be measured.
- **Placement.** These cells run on the `cs` partition under the `allcs`
  account, pinned to its A5000 nodes (node203, node204; node202 is draining).
  The 50 E78 cells are pinned to mltheory node302 (36 cells, A100) and node105
  (14 cells, A5000), and this campaign has already observed that terminal
  values cluster by GPU pool.
- **Evaluator.** The E78 record predates
  `eval_mode_coverage_disjoint_draws`, under which a prompt's consecutive
  evaluation draws share vLLM child streams and pooled mode-coverage statistics
  count repeats as independent. E126, E127, and E128 all take the corrected
  evaluator --- which matters here, because breadth is the metric the
  comparison is about.

E126, E127, and E128 share one runtime snapshot, one placement, one schedule,
and one evaluator, so the paired difference is taken entirely within this set.
Partition and account are audited from the scheduler's own record before
release, because a rerouted partition request is a failure this campaign has
hit before. When these rows appear in the alternatives table alongside UCPO and
RLEP-Dr, the table must state that their control differs.

No domain-specific coefficient, seed exclusion, early stopping choice, or
post-hoc replacement is permitted. A separate 32-query Graph/s43 learner smoke
is operational only and is excluded from every estimate. All scientific cells
depend on that smoke completing successfully.

## Outcomes and reporting

Primary outcomes are the existing campaign's terminal accuracy and verified
breadth metrics, including distinct correct modes at the registered K=8
evaluation and PMD. Report all five seeds and the paired seed-level effect; do
not select checkpoints by their outcome.

Also report GAPO's mechanism diagnostics: the mean distinct valid keys per
group, the support size range actually served, the fraction of groups whose
support exceeds the group size, and the realized frequency and reward ranges.
Any non-finite reward, any support lookup miss, and any verifier-positive row
reaching the reward without an answer key are mechanism failures.

## Failure rules

The immutable runtime must be byte-identical to the one E128 uses, and must
differ from its declared base snapshot only in the declared objective,
evaluator, and runner files. Submission is held until the
snapshot and scheduler exports are audited. A failed smoke blocks all 25
scientific jobs. A scientific cell may be retried only for an infrastructure
failure under the same frozen configuration; algorithmic failures remain data.
