# E49E singleton-repair cache-replay amendment

**Status: FROZEN BEFORE ANY SINGLETON-REPAIR JUDGE REQUEST OR POLICY TRAINING — 2026-07-24**

On restart and before materialization, a cached singleton-repair record is
accepted only after deterministic replay of its contract. Replay verifies the
row/problem/reference hashes, fixed proposal role and seed, completed response
identity, exact closed menu, answer/numeric leakage gates, both required
soundness roles and seeds, action-by-action assessment schema, conservative
MATH answer verification, and the first successful fixed slot. The embedded
menu must equal that recomputed first success byte-for-byte.

A stored `pass` bit is never sufficient. Any cache mismatch stops the stage;
it is not repaired by resampling.
