# E97 preregistration: UCPO on the Qwen2.5-0.5B collapse panel

Date frozen: 2026-08-12, before submission of any E97 scientific cell.

## Question and estimand

Does Uniform-Correct Policy Optimization (UCPO) preserve verified-answer
breadth better than the completed E78 Dr.GRPO compute-matched control while
retaining comparable accuracy? The estimand is the paired terminal-pass
difference, UCPO minus E78 control, within domain and seed. Intermediate
half-pass checkpoints are trajectories, not additional independent samples.

UCPO follows Lochab, Li, and Zhang (arXiv:2605.00365): for verifier-positive
rollouts it interpolates the sampled conditional-policy weighting with a
self-normalized inverse-probability estimate of uniform weighting, while
preserving each prompt group's total positive advantage mass. We fix the
paper's default tau=0.2 without tuning it on these domains.

## Frozen cohort

- Model and initialization: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, exactly as E78.
- Domains: Graph Coloring, Python Factors, and PantryPlan.
- Seeds: 43, 44, 45, 46, 47 (15 scientific cells).
- Training: 384 prompts, 8 passes, 16 rollouts per prompt, learning rate 2e-7,
  beta=0, one PPO epoch, and the E78 decoding/evaluation schedule.
- Placement, prompt surface, datasets, verifier, and all non-objective settings
  are inherited cell-by-cell from E78.
- Comparator: the already-completed E78 `control` cell for the same domain and
  seed. It is not re-run.
- Compute envelope: the passive, zero-derivative canonical replay traversal
  used by the E78 control remains enabled. UCPO's advantage redistribution is
  the only live-gradient difference.

No domain-specific coefficient, seed exclusion, early stopping choice, or
post-hoc replacement is permitted. A separate 32-query Graph/s43 learner smoke
is operational only and is excluded from every estimate. All scientific cells
depend on that smoke completing successfully.

## Outcomes and reporting

Primary outcomes are the existing campaign's terminal accuracy and verified
breadth metrics, including distinct correct modes at the registered K=8
evaluation. Report all five seeds and the paired seed-level effect; do not
select checkpoints by their outcome. Also report UCPO's eligible-group rate,
inverse-weight range, and maximum advantage-mass error. Any non-finite value or
mass error above the numerical tolerance is a mechanism failure.

## Failure rules

The immutable runtime must differ from the E78 snapshot only in the declared
UCPO/RLEP integration files and runner plumbing. Submission is held until the
snapshot and scheduler exports are audited. A failed smoke blocks all 15
scientific jobs. A scientific cell may be retried only for an infrastructure
failure under the same frozen configuration; algorithmic failures remain data.
