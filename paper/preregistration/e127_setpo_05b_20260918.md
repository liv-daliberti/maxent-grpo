# E127 preregistration: SetPO on the Qwen2.5-0.5B collapse panel

Date frozen: 2026-09-18, before any E127 scientific cell ran. This supersedes the
same-day `lambda = 0.1` freeze, whose cohort was withdrawn while every cell was
still PENDING; see the coefficient section below.

## Question and estimand

Does SetPO's set-level diversity shaping preserve verified-answer breadth
better than a matched Dr.GRPO compute-matched control while retaining
comparable accuracy? The estimand is the paired terminal-pass difference, SetPO
minus the E128 control (see below), within domain and seed. Intermediate half-pass checkpoints
are trajectories, not additional independent samples.

SetPO follows Li et al. (arXiv:2602.01062). For a rollout group `Omega`,

    D(Omega) = (1/|Omega|) sum_i g( (1/|Omega|) sum_j k(y_i, y_j) )

with `g(x) = -log(1 + x)` and `k` a cosine similarity between embedded
trajectories. Each row's leave-one-out marginal contribution is
`s_i = D(Omega) - D(Omega \ {o_i})`, and the shaped advantage is
`Ahat_i = Abar_i + lambda * s_i` with `lambda = 1.0`.

SetPO is the sharpest available test of something this campaign asserts
implicitly: that a certified outcome key is a better index of a mode than a
sentence embedding is. SetPO measures diversity in embedding space over
generated text; the replay methods and Semantic-MaxEnt measure it over
certified answer identity. Same panel, same control, a different notion of
"different".

## Three declared departures from the published setting

**The embedder is named.** The paper specifies "a pretrained embedding model"
without identifying one. These cells use
`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, loaded from a local snapshot through
the training environment's existing `transformers` install and mean-pooled over
the attention mask. Two reasons: it is the family DQO reports for the same
purpose, which keeps the two embedding-kernel methods in this comparison
commensurable; and it adds no package to a shared training environment that
other frozen admissions depend on. The weights are pinned by digest in the
cohort ledger. The embedder is inference-only and frozen; no gradient reaches
it, and the shaping term enters the advantage detached.

**The kernel is clamped onto `[0, 1]`.** `g` is undefined at `x <= -1`, and a
negative kernel mass would score a maximally dissimilar pair as worth less than
an empty set. Cosine similarity below zero is treated as zero similarity.

**`lambda` is registered against a measured scale, not tuned on an outcome.**
The paper reports no coefficient for a 0.5B verifier-reward setting.
`lambda = 1.0` is fixed before any scientific cell runs and is not revisited
afterwards. No domain-specific value is permitted, and no second value is run
inside this cohort.

The number is calibrated, not guessed, and the calibration is disclosed. An
earlier submission of this cohort at `lambda = 0.1` was withdrawn on the same
day, before any of its 25 scientific cells left PENDING. Its 32-query
operational smoke measured a shaping term of `marginal_abs_mean = 0.01279`
against a task advantage of `base_advantage_rms = 0.24206` --- 0.53% of the
Dr.GRPO advantage, small enough that the arm would most likely have been
indistinguishable from its control for reasons having nothing to do with the
method. `lambda = 1.0` places the term near 5.3% of the task advantage, which
is the ratio this campaign's own adaptive semantic controller targets
(`semantic_rms_target_ratio = 0.05`).

Only the smoke's mechanism diagnostics were consulted. No scientific cell had
run, no accuracy or breadth metric was read, and no run directory was created;
the withdrawn submission is retained under
`var/artifacts/withdrawn/` with its own record. If the realized shaping term at
`lambda = 1.0` still turns out to be negligible or overwhelming, that is a
reported result about the method at its registered setting, not grounds for a
third attempt.

The published rule adds the marginal term without group centering, and these
cells do the same. That leaves a non-zero group-mean shift in the shaped
advantage relative to the control; it is recorded per batch rather than
silently absorbed.

## Frozen cohort

- Model and initialization: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, exactly as E78.
- Domains: Graph Coloring, Countdown, PythonFactors, MathIR, PantryPlan.
- Seeds: 43, 44, 45, 46, 47 (25 scientific cells).
- Training: 384 prompts, 8 passes, 16 rollouts per prompt, learning rate 2e-7,
  beta=0, one PPO epoch, and the E78 decoding/evaluation schedule.
- Prompt surface, datasets, verifier, and all non-objective settings are
  inherited cell-by-cell from E78's registered configuration.
- Comparator: the E128 `control` cell for the same domain and seed, trained
  under this cohort's runtime, placement, and evaluator.
- Compute envelope: the passive, zero-derivative canonical replay traversal
  used by the E78 control remains enabled. SetPO's advantage shaping is the
  only live-gradient difference. The embedder's forward pass is additional
  compute on the training GPU and is not compute-matched to the control; it is
  small relative to the policy's rollout and update, and the departure is noted
  rather than compensated.

## The comparator is E128, not E78

These cells are differenced against **E128**, a matched Dr.GRPO control re-run
under this cohort's runtime, placement, and evaluator, not against the
completed E78 control that E97 and E115 used. Three things prevent inheriting
E78, and E128 removes all three:

- **Runtime.** E78 pins `e76_tuned_scale_96f68ebb47757af8`, retired by the
  irreversible 2026-09-04 cleanup of 528 unreferenced source snapshots and not
  reproduced by any commit in the repository's history. It cannot be rebuilt,
  and its distance from any present-day runtime cannot be measured.
- **Placement.** These cells run on the `cs` partition under the `allcs`
  account, pinned to its A5000 nodes (node203, node204; node202 is draining).
  The 50 E78 cells are pinned to mltheory node302 (36 cells, A100) and node105
  (14 cells, A5000), and this campaign has already observed that terminal
  values cluster by GPU pool.
- **Evaluator.** The E78 record predates
  `eval_mode_coverage_disjoint_draws`, under which a prompt's consecutive
  evaluation draws share vLLM child streams and pooled mode-coverage statistics
  count repeats as independent. E126, E127, and E128 all take the corrected
  evaluator --- which matters here, because breadth is the metric the
  comparison is about.

E126, E127, and E128 share one runtime snapshot, one placement, one schedule,
and one evaluator, so the paired difference is taken entirely within this set.
Partition and account are audited from the scheduler's own record before
release, because a rerouted partition request is a failure this campaign has
hit before. When these rows appear in the alternatives table alongside UCPO and
RLEP-Dr, the table must state that their control differs.

No domain-specific coefficient, seed exclusion, early stopping choice, or
post-hoc replacement is permitted. A separate 32-query Graph/s43 learner smoke
is operational only and is excluded from every estimate. All scientific cells
depend on that smoke completing successfully.

## Outcomes and reporting

Primary outcomes are the existing campaign's terminal accuracy and verified
breadth metrics, including distinct correct modes at the registered K=8
evaluation and PMD. Report all five seeds and the paired seed-level effect; do
not select checkpoints by their outcome.

Also report SetPO's mechanism diagnostics: the kernel's realized range, the
mean set diversity, the marginal contribution range, the absolute group-mean
shift the uncentred term introduces, and the base and shaped advantage RMS.
Any non-finite embedding, marginal, or shaped advantage is a mechanism failure.
An embedder snapshot that cannot be loaded offline is a submission failure, not
a run-time fallback.

## Failure rules

The immutable runtime must be byte-identical to the one E128 uses, and must
differ from its declared base snapshot only in the declared objective,
evaluator, and runner files. Submission is held until the
snapshot and scheduler exports are audited. A failed smoke blocks all 25
scientific jobs. A scientific cell may be retried only for an infrastructure
failure under the same frozen configuration; algorithmic failures remain data.
