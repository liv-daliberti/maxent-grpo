# Reference authenticity and usage audit — 2026-09-05

Checked **93 bibliography entries**, including **28 originally cited sources** across both papers. All 93 correspond to identifiable sources; no nonexistent work was found. The revised papers cite **29 sources**, after adding the already-listed Sinha et al. predecessor to the theory discussion. **9 bibliography records** were corrected or pinned to a consistent version.

“Real” does not mean every claim in the source is correct. This audit checks source identity, metadata, and whether the manuscript fairly describes the source; it does not independently reproduce the cited experiments or certify every external theorem.

## Remaining verification limits

- **Info-GRPO is unused.** Its matching anonymous OpenReview paper exists, but direct access was blocked. Current authorship, publication decision, and original posting date remain unverified. Its record is retained as an anonymous submission rather than promoted to a confirmed publication.
- **Hofbauer and Sigmund:** Cambridge confirms the book and replicator-dynamics chapter, but the chapter body was unavailable. The standard-form equation identification was checked independently; the manuscript provides its own specialization proof.
- **Dang et al. is cited narrowly.** The official workshop program, abstract, and indexed primary passages confirm identity and the broad diversity observation; the complete OpenReview PDF could not be opened. No detailed theorem or numerical claim depends on inaccessible text.
- The two MAA competition pages exist but expose no verified 2024 publication date. Both now use **n.d.** and a September5,2026 access date.
