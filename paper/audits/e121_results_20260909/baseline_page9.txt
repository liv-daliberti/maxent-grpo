452   8     C ONCLUSION
453
454   M ODE B ENCH makes a missing part of evaluation measurable: the range of distinct, verified
455   outcomes a model can produce for one problem. Accuracy tells us whether a model can suc-
456   ceed; diversity helps describe the alternatives still available to it. That distinction matters be-
457   cause different approaches can have different strengths. One may succeed where another fails,
458   or remain useful when the problem or its constraints change. Knowing several ways to solve
459   could therefore be valuable even when a familiar approach already works.
460   Our experiments show that replay complements learning from fresh samples. Remembering
461   verified solutions improves held-out correctness and preserves more correct output alternatives
462   across the completed comparisons. M ODE B ENCH lets us measure those alternatives through
463   executable, task-defined identities. It provides a controlled starting point for understanding
464   what training preserves, alongside what training learns to solve.
