\noindent\textbf{A motivating test on hosted models.}
Table~\ref{tab:frontier-motivation} evaluates the same five domains
at all three levels without additional training. On Level-3 Graph,
$99.8--100.0\%$ of answers are correct, yet $92.7--99.4\%$ of correct pairs repeat a mode
(uniform reference: $19.0\%$). This motivates measuring breadth alongside accuracy;
it does not identify a training cause or a model-size effect.
