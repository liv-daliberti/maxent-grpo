\noindent\textbf{Hosted accuracy and output concentration.}
\label{sec:hosted-concentration}
The same inference-only test evaluates 6 hosted deployments on five domains
and three levels (128 prompts per cell, eight stateless responses each; 15,360
responses per deployment). On Level-3 Graph, strict correctness is
$99.8--100.0\%$, while $92.7--99.4\%$ of correct pairs repeat a coloring
($1.02--1.23$ distinct modes per eight draws). Figure~\ref{fig:hosted-graph-comparison}
and Appendix~\ref{app:hosted-comparison} compare all completed deployments.
These static output distributions identify neither a training cause nor a
model-size effect; provider reasoning labels do not establish matched compute.
