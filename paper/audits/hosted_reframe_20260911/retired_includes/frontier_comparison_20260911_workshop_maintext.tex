\noindent\textbf{Hosted accuracy and output concentration.}
\label{sec:hosted-concentration}
The same inference-only test evaluates 6 hosted deployments (15,360 responses each).
Level-3 Graph has $99.8--100.0\%$ strict correctness, yet $92.7--99.4\%$ of correct
pairs repeat a coloring ($1.02--1.23$ modes per eight draws). This does not
identify a training cause; all models and the prompt and level limitations
appear in Appendix~\ref{app:hosted-comparison}.
