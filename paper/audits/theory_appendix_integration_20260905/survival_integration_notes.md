## Mathematical and measurement checks

The result is an application of established information-theoretic/statistical tools, not a claim to a new foundational inequality or to verified neural-optimizer descent. The fragment separates a certificate at one checkpoint from an energy bound valid over a training trajectory.

- **Sharp normalized KL certificate.** Extending normalized target weights by zero gives `KL(w_extended || p) = R_w - H(w)`. Binary log-sum yields the sharp lower inverse, with the complementary target masses proportional at equality. All-bank mass, binary Pinsker, and a positive elementary exponential bound are proved. The one-outcome alphabet is explicitly trivial. For strictly positive off-bank probabilities, the sharp boundary construction is approached only for positive excess budget; zero excess forces `p = w_extended` exactly.
- **Entropy threshold.** The alphabet is a known finite set of possible modes, allowing zero coordinates. The coordinate-entropy envelope gives a positive floor exactly above `log(m-1)`, with equality constructions included. The case of a coordinate equal to one is handled without forming an undefined conditional distribution. This is a level-set comparison, not a prediction of collapse under exact entropy dynamics.
- **Complete-response mapping.** Exemplars are distinct disjoint complete events at one prompt and checkpoint. Invalid/other outcomes, and nontermination when needed, make the policy alphabet normalized. Grouping multiple exemplars by their deterministic execution key and then applying the binary partition gives the key certificate. EOS or another terminal event/fixed horizon is required. Prefix likelihood and mean-token likelihood are distinguished from complete-sequence probability. Prompt formatting, temperature, masks, top-p/top-k, truncation, verifier, and checkpoint must match the target sampling law.
- **Length normalization.** With `a_j = alpha_j/L_j`, `A = sum a_j`, and `w_j = a_j/A`, a loss ceiling `D_0` yields normalized cross-entropy ceiling `C = D_0/A`. Multiple prompts require separate conditional calculations or an explicit joint prompt mixture. Finite approximate scores require numerical-error control before they become rigorous numerical certificates.
- **Statistical scope.** Named keys and frozen-policy draws are prespecified; zero sightings give no positive lower bound or extinction proof. The quoted numerical limits are hypothetical, separate one-sided limits. Optional stopping is covered by the fixed-policy likelihood-ratio mixture, not by pooling changing policies. Error allocation can cover multiple prespecified prompt/key/checkpoint statements without independence between keys. Observations at checkpoints alone say nothing about unobserved intervening states. Deterministic teacher-forced scores are not Bernoulli observations.
