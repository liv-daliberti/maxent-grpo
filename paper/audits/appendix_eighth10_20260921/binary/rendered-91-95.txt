4865   For a fixed prompt, the binary-reward score estimators defined below have mean updates along the
4866   correctness gradient, while their sampling covariances can differ. With independently optimized
4867   categorical logits, these updates amplify differences among equally rewarded modes, and sampling
4868   can break an initially exact symmetry. Fixed-bank replay supplies probability floors, restoration
4869   thresholds, and recovery bounds under the stated update assumptions. Neural retention requires
4870   additional conditions on complete-response likelihoods and local updates. These results do not
4871   certify the implemented AdamW/PPO trajectories. Table 2 defines the notation.
