\noindent\textbf{A motivating hosted-model test.}
A held-out test shows why verified alternatives should be measured alongside
accuracy: both hosted deployments answer correctly on every draw, yet repeated
samples yield almost one distinct coloring per prompt
(Table~\ref{tab:frontier-motivation}).
