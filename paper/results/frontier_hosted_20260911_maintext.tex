\noindent\textbf{A hosted model also concentrates verified outputs.}
\label{sec:hosted-concentration}
An exploratory, inference-only GPT-5.6 Sol evaluation covers five domains and
three test levels (128 prompts per cell, eight stateless samples each; 15,360
responses). Level-3 Graph has $100\%$ correctness but only $1.16$ distinct
colorings per eight samples: $95.1\%$ of correct pairs repeat a coloring,
versus a $19.0\%$ uniform-correct-key reference. No formatting correction is
needed. Domain trends across levels are nonmonotonic; these observations do
not identify a training cause or model-size effect. Appendix~\ref{app:hosted-concentration}
gives all domains, the exploratory Level-3 provenance, and prompt limitations.
