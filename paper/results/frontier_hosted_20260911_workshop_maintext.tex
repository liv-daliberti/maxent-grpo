\noindent\textbf{Concentration also appears in a hosted model.}
\label{sec:hosted-concentration}
An inference-only GPT-5.6 Sol evaluation covers five domains and three test
levels (15,360 responses). Level-3 Graph has $100\%$ correctness but only
$1.16$ distinct colorings per eight samples: $95.1\%$ of correct pairs repeat
one coloring. This does not identify a training cause; level trends and prompt
confounds appear in Appendix~\ref{app:hosted-concentration}.
