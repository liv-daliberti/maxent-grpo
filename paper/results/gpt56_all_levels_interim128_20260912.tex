\subsection{Interim view of all three levels}
\label{app:gpt56-all-levels-interim}

Figure~\ref{fig:gpt56-sampling-budget} contains all five domains at
Levels~1, 2, and~3: sixteen fixed problems per cell, 240 problems in total.
The common 128-response budget includes 30,720 authenticated responses.
The fixed follow-up to 512 responses per problem is still being collected;
this explicitly labeled interim figure does not display unfinished budgets.

Within each problem, the interim sample follows the frozen HTTP-request
manifest order across collection stages. Selection uses the first 128
planned requests, independently of their answers or completion order.
Native global draw indices and their interim ordering remain in bound
selection files. Every selected request has a completed native receipt,
matching prompt bytes, generation controls, and the provider-reported
\texttt{gpt-5.6-sol-2026-07-09} snapshot. Original wording, medium reasoning,
an 8,192-token output limit, and omitted temperature and top-$p$ are fixed.

Curves use exact rarefaction within each complete 128-response pool and
weight all sixteen problems equally. Pointwise 95\% intervals use 20,000
whole-problem bootstrap replicates within domain and level. The frozen
formatting normalizer supplies the main display; strict grades and every
failure remain in the accompanying JSON. Graph references are exact;
other references are certified lower bounds and may be exceeded. The
finite-budget curves cannot establish asymptotic saturation or exclude
additional rare modes beyond the completed sampling budget.
