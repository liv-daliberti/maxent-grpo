\subsection{Hosted discovery: guidance, formatting, and late modes}
\label{app:hosted-discovery-complete}

The completed hosted panel contains 36,864 fresh responses from GPT-5.4,
GPT-5.6 Sol, and Grok 4.3: sixteen fixed problems in each of six
Python/MathIR/Pantry level cells, two wordings, and 64 draws per condition.
The full report retains both grading conventions, all observed failures,
ordered-prefix sensitivities, and every conditional-eligibility denominator.
These fixed deployment configurations form a separate experiment from the
reasoning-disabled main table. The historical Level-3 problems also precede
the newly admitted neutral-Python dataset; this follow-up evaluates no
checkpoint trained on that successor dataset.

\paragraph{MathIR guidance changes GPT's correct-solution breadth.}
At Level~2, both GPT deployments produce at most one verified mode per
problem under original wording even with 64 draws. At matched budgets of
eight correct draws, neutral wording increases mean breadth by $.794$
($[.623,.990]$) for GPT-5.4 and $.872$ ($[.606,1.123]$) for GPT-5.6 Sol.
The Level-3 effects are $.204$ ($[.106,.320]$) and $.860$
($[.563,1.141]$). Grok's corresponding intervals include zero at both levels.
The GPT results therefore survive conditioning on observed correctness;
Grok's intervals leave its corresponding effect unresolved. These
pointwise exploratory intervals resample the sixteen paired problems,
without replication across hosted model training seeds.

\paragraph{Pantry continues discovering modes after first success.}
Under neutral wording, GPT-5.4 gains $2.583$ and $3.219$ verified modes
between eight and 64 draws at Levels~2 and~3; Grok gains $3.377$ and
$4.466$. Grok's empirical pass probability is already $1.0$ at eight draws
in both cells, so these gains consist entirely of additional modes.
At Level~3 its distinct count rises from $3.972$ to $8.438$.
At eight correct draws, neutral minus original breadth is $1.781$
($[1.226,2.338]$) for Grok and $.934$ ($[.670,1.184]$) for GPT-5.4;
GPT-5.6 Sol's Level-3 contrast is inconclusive. These observed counts
measure finite-sample discovery rather than exhaustive solution coverage.

\paragraph{Python requires the formatting sensitivity.}
GPT-5.6 Sol's original-wording strict pass probability remains $.1875$
at Level~2 and $.0625$ at Level~3 from eight through 64 draws. The frozen
formatting normalizer instead gives $P_{64}=1.0$ under both wordings at
both levels, with all four $P_8$ values above $.989$. Thus its low strict
scores cannot be interpreted as an absence of valid mathematical solutions.
The normalized neutral-minus-original increase in late mode discovery is
$.681$ ($[.351,1.066]$) at Level~2 and $.370$ ($[.112,.674]$) at Level~3.
Both the strict failure pattern and the normalized sensitivity remain
visible; formatting rescue is not a change to the verifier or mode key.
